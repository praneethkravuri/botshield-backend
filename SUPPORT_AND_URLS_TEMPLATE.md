# Support And URL Template

Fill these in before Shopify submission.

## Production URLs

- Production app URL: `https://botshield.your-domain.com`
- Auth redirect URL: `https://botshield.your-domain.com/auth/callback`
- Support URL: `https://botshield.your-domain.com/support`
- Privacy policy URL: `https://botshield.your-domain.com/privacy`
- Optional terms URL: `https://botshield.your-domain.com/terms`

## Shopify config fields to update

In `shopify.app.toml` replace:

- `application_url`
- `redirect_urls`
